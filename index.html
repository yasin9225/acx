About ACX

ACX is a utility token deployed on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). It is designed to provide structured access to digital services, platform features, and ecosystem participation within the ACX and Art City environment.

ACX is created strictly for functional usage and service enablement. It does not represent equity, ownership, or investment rights.

• Platform access authorization • Service activation and verification • Digital ecosystem participation • On-chain identity linkage
